INTRODUCTION TO AI AND MACHINE LEARNING – INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on developing machines or systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making.

Examples: ChatGPT, virtual assistants, facial recognition, and self-driving cars.

2. What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of Artificial Intelligence that enables computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every situation.

Example: An email spam filter learns from previously labelled emails to identify whether a new email is spam or not.

3. Difference Between Rule-Based AI and ML-Based AI

Rule-Based AI:

* Works using predefined rules written by humans.
* Follows explicit if-then conditions.
* Does not automatically learn from new data.
* Best suited for well-defined problems.
* Example: A rule-based chatbot.

ML-Based AI:

* Learns patterns from data.
* Uses a trained model to make predictions.
* Can improve through training with relevant data.
* Useful for complex problems where patterns are difficult to specify manually.
* Example: An email spam classifier trained on data.

In short: Rule-based AI follows predefined rules, whereas ML-based AI learns patterns from data.

4. Difference Between Supervised and Unsupervised Machine Learning

Supervised Learning:

* Uses labelled data.
* Correct outputs are provided during training.
* Learns to predict outputs for new data.
* Main tasks include classification and regression.
* Example: Predicting house prices using historical data.

Unsupervised Learning:

* Uses unlabelled data.
* Correct outputs are not provided.
* Finds hidden patterns or structures in data.
* Main tasks include clustering and dimensionality reduction.
* Example: Grouping customers based on purchasing behaviour.

In short: Supervised learning learns from labelled examples, while unsupervised learning discovers patterns in unlabelled data.

5. Difference Between Strong AI and Weak AI

Weak AI (Narrow AI):

* Designed to perform specific tasks.
* Operates within a limited domain.
* Exists in practical applications today.
* Example: Voice assistants and recommendation systems.

Strong AI:

* Refers to AI with general, human-like intelligence and understanding.
* Would handle a wide range of intellectual tasks.
* Has not been achieved and remains a theoretical concept.
* Example: A hypothetical machine with general human-level intelligence.

In short: Weak AI performs specific tasks, whereas Strong AI would possess general intelligence comparable to human intelligence.

6. What Are the Applications of Artificial Intelligence?

7. Healthcare: Disease detection, medical image analysis, and drug discovery.

8. Education: Personalized learning, automated grading, and AI tutors.

9. Finance: Fraud detection, credit risk assessment, and algorithmic trading.

10. Transportation: Self-driving technology, traffic prediction, and route optimization.

11. E-commerce: Product recommendations, chatbots, and demand forecasting.

12. Cybersecurity: Detecting suspicious activities and potential cyberattacks.

13. Natural Language Processing: Language translation, text summarization, and chatbots.

14. Entertainment: Movie, music, and content recommendations.

Conclusion:
AI helps automate tasks, analyze large amounts of data, improve decision-making, and provide personalized services.
